ABOUT US

BBF Rentals (B&B Formwork Division) is a trusted provider of construction equipment rental and materials supply, committed to delivering high-quality, reliable solutions for the building industry.

Formed as an internal facility of B&B Builders, the Formwork Division was originally created to meet in-house requirements for infrastructure, machinery, and plant equipment. Over time, recognizing the demand in the wider market, we evolved into a full-service rental and supply business, catering not only to our own projects but also to external construction companies.
