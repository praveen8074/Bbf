ABOUT US

BBF Rentals (B&B Formwork Division) is a trusted provider of construction equipment rental and materials supply, committed to delivering high-quality, reliable solutions for the building industry. Formed as an internal facility of B&B Builders, the Formwork Division was originally created to meet in-house requirements for infrastructure, machinery, and plant equipment. Over time, recognizing the demand in the wider market, we evolved into a full-service rental and supply business, catering not only to our own projects but also to external construction companies.

Maximize Your Budget With Equipment Rentals

Renting construction equipment helps you cut costs without compromising on quality. Instead of investing heavily in buying machines, you only pay for what you need, when you need it. This means no maintenance expenses, no storage worries, and no depreciation losses. With rentals, you get access to well-maintained, reliable equipment that keeps your projects on schedule—while freeing up your budget for other critical needs like materials, labor, or technology upgrades.

Cost Efficiency

Choosing equipment rentals is a smart way to maximize resources. Instead of tying up capital in costly purchases, you only pay for equipment when it’s needed—making projects more affordable and flexible.

Smart Choice

Rentals reduce maintenance, storage, and repair expenses, while giving you access to the latest, well-maintained machinery. It’s a cost-efficient, risk-free choice that helps you focus on building smarter, faster, and within budget.
